Before we close, the AppliedIQ Angle. Today's thread is price, and the price that matters is the one you pay for a finished task, not for a token. Muse Wonder's figure of about 4.52 dollars per task is an evaluator's number on a benchmark. It is not your workflow. The useful version is smaller. If you run a workflow on a model, pick one, such as an order-exception summary or a bill-of-materials check. Count how many runs finished correctly last month, then divide the model spend by that count. That is cost per finished task, and it is the only number that lets you compare two providers fairly.

Here is the analogy, flagged as mine. This is landed cost, not unit price. A supplier quoting a low per-kilo rate can still be the expensive one once you count the rework.
